Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Flavour

At Pho Vietnam 3969, we bring the vibrant tastes of Vietnam to Niagara Falls. Every bowl of pho is simmered for hours to achieve that rich, aromatic broth our guests crave.

From our famous Pho Dau Bo to crisp spring rolls and lemongrass chicken, each dish is made with fresh ingredients and generations-old recipes.
